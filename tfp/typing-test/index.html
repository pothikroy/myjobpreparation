Good to know

  • Net WPM counts only correct characters on screen: correct characters ÷ 5 ÷ minutes. Gross WPM counts every keystroke: keystrokes ÷ 5 ÷ minutes.
  • Accuracy = correct keystrokes ÷ total keystrokes. Mistakes you later fix still count against it.
  • Accented letters (é, è, ç) can be typed as plain e, e, c. The bullet • accepts * or -, and a tab accepts Tab or Space.
  • The test pauses if you switch tabs or windows. Unfinished tests are not saved.
